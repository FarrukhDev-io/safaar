## 1-qism: Parol xavfsizligi (HIGH bug)

Audit natijasida topilgan "minimal parol uzunligi yo'q, login uchun maxsus
throttle yo'q, account lockout yo'q" muammosi tuzatildi:

- **Parol kuchliligi** — `CompleteProfileDto`, `UserResetPasswordDto`,
  `ResetPasswordDto`ga kamida 8 belgi + harf+raqam talabi qo'shildi
  (`@IsStrongPassword()` composed validator).
- **Throttle** — barcha login/OTP/parol-tiklash endpointlariga maxsus
  `@Throttle` qo'shildi (login: 10/daq, OTP so'rov: 5/daq).
- **Account lockout** — `userLogin`, `partnerLogin`, `adminLogin`ga
  `AppCacheService` orqali muvaffaqiyatsiz urinishlar hisoblagichi
  qo'shildi: 5 marta noto'g'ri parol → hisob 15 daqiqaga bloklanadi.

22 ta yangi test qo'shildi, barchasi production'da live tasdiqlandi (weak
password → 400, 6-urinishda `AUTH_ACCOUNT_LOCKED`, throttle → 429).
